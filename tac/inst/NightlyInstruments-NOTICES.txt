TAC conversion: selected recordings converted to 32 kHz PCM16 FLAC. Original channel count
is retained except for the steel guitar. Samples use finite waveforms and gate note-off
envelopes. Long recordings get a terminal fade where truncated. TAC definitions specify
pitch regions, small tuning corrections and gain adjustment. Distinct recorded velocity
layers are mapped where listed in the definitions. No product or work affiliation
or endorsement is implied.

Exact source sample IDs, file hashes, mapping and processing are in sample-provenance.json
in the source-side asset verification record.
